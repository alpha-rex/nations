How nations began

Twenty-five countries, each told from scratch: who was there first, what shaped it, and what it handed to the world. Pick one.

Tap a tile to open a country. Tiles sit roughly where each country is on the globe; they are not drawn to scale.

Ordered by each nation's founding moment, oldest first. Scroll sideways for more. Choosing that moment is a judgement call; the timeline shows the longer story.

A short overview, not a full history. Dates are the commonly cited ones; many of these stories are still debated by historians.
